Why Visit Mindo From Quito?

Mindo is one of the closest cloud forest destinations to Quito and offers a completely different atmosphere from the city. In only a few hours, travelers can move from the high Andes into lush green cloud forest landscapes filled with hummingbirds, toucans, butterflies, orchids, waterfalls, and tropical vegetation.

For travelers with limited time in Ecuador, Mindo is one of the best ways to experience biodiversity without domestic flights, complicated jungle logistics, or an overnight stay.

Schedule and transport questions

Which departure should I choose?

Choose 3 AM for the 5 AM dawn or full-day routes, 4 AM for Jewels of the Morning Forest from 6–10 AM, or 9 AM for the hummingbird tour from 11 AM–2 PM. We confirm pickup timing for your address.

Is Quito transportation included?

Quito roundtrip transportation is quoted separately from tours starting in Mindo. When you select a Quito package with transport, it is included in that package’s quoted total. Your emailed quote confirms inclusions.

Can we return after the night walk?

Yes, with advance planning. The walk starts at 7 PM and lasts 2–2.5 hours, so the return drive begins after the walk, around 9–9:30 PM and Quito arrival is later that night.

What should I send to request a plan?

Send your date, pickup location, group size, preferred birding tour, optional activities, mobility needs and desired return time. Enquire in advance through WhatsApp, email or the tour request form.
