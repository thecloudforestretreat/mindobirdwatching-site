Stay details to know

Confirmed by the host
  • Minimum stay: one night. Check-in is 15:00 and checkout is 11:00.
  • Children age five and older are welcome. Pets, smoking, and events are not permitted.
  • Quiet hours run from 22:00 to 08:00. Entry includes stairs in a forest setting.
  • There is no luggage storage or complimentary lodging for guides and drivers.

Birding and transport

Coordinate before arrival
  • Guests may walk the main birding road independently and can come and go early.
  • Use a taxi for reserves or town if you are not driving; MINDOLOFT can also coordinate town transfer in/out.
  • Parking is available, and Mindo Bird Watching vehicles and guides may enter the property.
  • Quito and airport transfers are external services and require advance confirmation.

MINDOLOFT planning questions

How many guests can stay at MINDOLOFT?

The loft accommodates up to three guests. It has a queen bed and a sofa bed in one private 60 m² space.

Can birders leave before sunrise?

Yes. Guests control their access and may walk the main birding road independently. A taxi is the practical choice for early trips to reserves or into Mindo town.

Is breakfast available before an early birding tour?

Yes. Ingredients for a full vegetarian breakfast are provided for guests to prepare at any hour.

How are reservations and cancellations handled?

Reservations are confirmed by email. Bookings made at least 30 days ahead require a 50% deposit, with the balance due 15 days before arrival; bookings within 30 days require full payment. Cancellations made more than five days before arrival receive a 70% refund. Within five days and for no-shows, payments are nonrefundable.

Can Mindo Bird Watching arrange a tour from MINDOLOFT?

Yes. Share your dates, experience, target birds, and transport needs so we can plan a private route and practical pickup.
