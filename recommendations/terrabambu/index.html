TerraBambú planning FAQs

Is TerraBambú both a lodge and a restaurant?

Yes. It combines lodging and an on-site restaurant, which can make meals and quieter evenings easier during a Mindo stay.

Can I pair a stay with a private birdwatching tour?

Yes. Mindo Bird Watching can plan a route around your dates, experience level, target birds, and pickup needs.

What should I confirm before an early birding tour?

Confirm pickup access, breakfast timing, your guide’s meeting point, and any transport needs the day before.

Where should I verify rates and availability?

Use TerraBambú’s website or WhatsApp for current room availability, restaurant hours, rates, and policies.
