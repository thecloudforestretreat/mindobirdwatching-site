FAQ

Cloud Forest Tour Questions

Do you show prices on this page?

No. We send a personalized quote after reviewing your dates, group size, duration and pickup needs. No payment is required to submit an inquiry.

Can I combine birding with a night walk?

Yes. Morning birding and a night walk work well when you stay overnight in Mindo. A same-day Quito trip usually returns before the night walk begins.

Are spectacled bear sightings guaranteed?

No. Spectacled bears are wild animals. Recent activity, weather, route access and animal movement influence every search.

Can you arrange pickup from Quito?

Yes. Share your Quito or Mindo pickup location in the form and we will explain suitable transportation and timing options.
