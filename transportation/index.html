Public transport from Quito Airport to Mindo

There is no verified direct public bus from UIO to Mindo. This route requires two connections.

1. UIO → CarcelénPublic bus: locally reported at $2.50 and about 1 hour.
2. Carcelén → La OfeliaTaxi: approximately $3–$4 and about 10–20 minutes.
3. La Ofelia → MindoFlor del Valle: $3.60 and about 2–2.5 hours after departure.

Planning estimate for one person: approximately $9.10–$10.10 and 3½–4½ hours, depending mainly on the connection at La Ofelia. If your flight arrives after 14:00, the 16:00 final bus can be difficult to reach after immigration, baggage collection and transfers. Prices and times are locally supplied estimates, not guaranteed fares.

Where to catch the bus to Mindo

Flor del Valle, Terminal La Ofelia, Av. Diego de Vásquez and Los Ciruelos, opposite Liga Deportiva Universitaria's stadium.

Using Uber between Quito and Mindo

Uber operates in Quito and supports UIO pickups, but Mindo is not listed as an Uber service city.

Check before relying on it

Request the route in the app and wait for a driver to accept. The price changes with distance, time, demand, tolls and the selected vehicle. For a time-sensitive flight or return from Mindo, arrange a backup rather than assuming a car will be available.

Use the in-app safety checks

Before entering, match the license plate, vehicle make and model, and driver photo with the app. Ask the driver to confirm your name, wear a seat belt and share the trip status with someone you trust.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the Quito to Mindo bus leave?

The direct Flor del Valle bus leaves from Terminal La Ofelia in north Quito, not Terminal Carcelén.

Can I take Uber from Quito to Mindo?

You can request the trip in Quito or at UIO, but the ride exists only after a driver accepts it. Do not assume that an Uber will be available for the return from Mindo.

Can I reach Mindo by bus after an afternoon flight?

It may be difficult. The final listed La Ofelia departure is 16:00, and the airport route requires a bus to Carcelén plus a taxi to La Ofelia.
