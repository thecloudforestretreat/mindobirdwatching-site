Western Emerald

The Western Emerald (Chlorostilbon melanorhynchus) is a small hummingbird of western Ecuador and Colombia. If it is on your target list, start with its habitat and ask about recent observations before choosing a route.

Before choosing a route

Do not choose a garden solely from a species list. Ask about recent observations, current conditions and a sensible alternative if the bird does not appear.

Field marks and habitat

Male

Iridescent green plumage, a dark blue tail and a short, straight bill.

Female

Green upperparts and pale gray underparts, with a white line behind the eye contrasting with the dark cheek.

Habitat

Look into suitable gardens, forest edges and open areas. Different hummingbirds do not all use the same habitats.

Watch and photograph with care

Watch from a distance that lets the bird feed and move freely. Leave feeders and flowers unobstructed, avoid chasing a bird for a photograph and follow site guidance. Before a photography outing, explain your equipment and how much time you want at each stop.

Identification reference

Identification and habitat context are based on the Western Emerald — eBird / Cornell Lab species account. Consult its images and range information; presence at a particular site needs recent observations.

Frequently asked questions

Is a sighting guaranteed on a tour?

No. No tour guarantees a particular species. Ask about recent observations and agree a flexible route before booking.

Is the hummingbird tour right if this is my priority?

The dedicated tour focuses on enjoying several hummingbird species. If Western Emerald is your main target, first ask whether conditions and the route fit; a custom private outing may be more suitable.

What should I send the guide?

Share your date, available time, starting point, target list, experience and mobility or photography needs.

Can I use this page to identify a photograph?

Use it as a starting point. Compare several field marks and consult eBird images and references; a photograph taken in poor light can hide useful details.
