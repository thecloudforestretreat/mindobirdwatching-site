Prepare for changing cloud forest conditions

What to bring

Pack light, but protect yourself and your equipment from sun, rain and damp trails. Your exact equipment inclusions depend on the confirmed booking.

    ☁
    Weather and clothing

    Expect quick changes

    Mindo can move between sun, mist and rain in the same outing. Dress in light layers, carry rain protection and keep electronics in a dry bag. Your guide may adjust the route for current conditions.

      Delays and emergencies

      Tour delay or meeting issue

      Stay at the agreed meeting point and message your guide first. If the guide cannot be reached, contact Mindo Bird Watching.

      Immediate emergency in Ecuador

      Call Ecuador’s single emergency number and clearly share your location and what happened.
