Planning questions

Does the rainfall average predict rain on my tour date?

No. It is a twenty-year regional reference. Check the forecast and access conditions close to departure; terrain can change rainfall substantially between stops.

Can I use this figure to choose a target species?

No. Rainfall is not a measure of species presence. Share your targets and review recent records with your guide before choosing a route.
