Sachaguatusa Reserve Birding near Mindo, Ecuador

Sachaguatusa Reserve, listed on eBird as Sacha Guatusa Reserva Natural, is a birding stop in the broader Mashpi area of Pichincha. Recent birding sources highlight feeder-based viewing, lower-elevation Chocó specialties and strong photography potential, especially for tanagers, hummingbirds, toucans and barbets.

Quick answer: Is Sachaguatusa Reserve worth visiting from Mindo?

Yes, particularly for lower-elevation Chocó birds and feeder photography. The verified eBird location and recent birding reports support active tanager, hummingbird and toucan use.
