Double Extreme Swing FAQs

Practical answers before you add the swing to your Mindo day.

How long does the Double Extreme Swing take?

The actual swing is brief. Allow extra time for arrival, safety briefing, equipment checks, waiting, and the current operator flow.

Is the Double Extreme Swing difficult?

It is not physically demanding, but it is best for people who are comfortable with height, motion, and a fast adrenaline experience.

Can children do the Double Extreme Swing?

Older children and teens may be able to participate, but current age, height, weight, harness, and operator rules must be confirmed first.

Can the swing run in the rain?

Light rain may be possible, but stronger rain, wind, poor visibility, or unsafe conditions can affect timing or availability.

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable clothing and secure closed shoes. Avoid loose items and confirm how phones or cameras should be stored or used.

Can I combine the swing with birdwatching?

Yes. Keep early morning for birds, then use the swing later in the morning or afternoon as a short adventure add-on.
