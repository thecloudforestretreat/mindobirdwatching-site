Where we look for Andean bears

The exact route is selected close to the tour date instead of promising one fixed site.

Antisana area

High-Andean landscapes may offer bear-searching opportunities together with strong possibilities for Andean Condors, raptors, deer and other páramo wildlife.

Chosen when: recent activity, road access and weather make this the stronger option.

Cayambe-Coca area

Cloud forest and high-Andean habitat can support a different search strategy, with the guide adapting observation points to current reports and field conditions.

Chosen when: activity, visibility, access and travel logistics favor this route.

How your route is selected

Your guide considers recent wildlife activity, weather, visibility, road conditions, pickup location, tour duration and the interests of your group.

What we never promise

We do not imply that bears live in Mindo, guarantee a sighting, or force a route when conditions are unsuitable. The experience is built around responsible observation of wild animals.

Responsible viewing and realistic expectations

The quality of a wildlife experience is measured by responsible decisions, not by forcing a sighting.

Patient observation

We use distance, optics and quiet scanning rather than approaching, feeding or disturbing wildlife. Your guide may change or stop an observation when animal welfare or field safety requires it.

No sighting guarantee

Spectacled bears are shy, mobile and influenced by food, weather and habitat conditions. A well-planned tour improves the opportunity to search effectively, but it cannot control wild behavior.

Frequently asked questions

Direct answers for planning your private bear experience.

What does the private tour include?

An experienced guide, private transportation, binoculars and a spotting scope. Route selection is based on recent activity, access, weather and field conditions.

Is a half-day or full-day tour better?

The extended half day suits tighter schedules. The full day provides more time to wait, adjust the route and include broader wildlife observation, so it is our recommended format.

Can you pick me up in Quito or Mindo?

Yes. Round trips and one-way Mindo-Quito or Quito-Mindo transfers can be arranged around your accommodation and next destination.

Are spectacled bear sightings guaranteed?

No. These are wild, shy animals. Your guide plans the strongest practical route, but no bear or other wildlife sighting can be guaranteed.

Can I combine birding in Mindo with a bear tour?

Yes. We can organize one private Mindo birding day and a separate bear and high-Andean wildlife day, including flexible transport between Mindo and Quito.

What should I bring?

Bring warm layers, rain protection, comfortable walking footwear, water, sun protection, snacks or lunch as advised, and your camera. Binoculars and a spotting scope are provided.
