Tubing is the closest substitute for travelers who want a water activity. It is generally easier to fit into a Mindo itinerary and works well for groups seeking fun without a technical rafting outing.

Waterfalls and canyoning preserve a strong sense of adventure centered on cloud forest scenery. They are good options for travelers who want movement, water, and memorable photographs.
