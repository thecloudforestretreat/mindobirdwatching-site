Birdwatching itinerary questions

How many days are best for birdwatching in Mindo?

One day provides a strong introduction. Two days is our recommended short plan. Three days gives target birders more habitat and flexibility. Four days adds the Andean Highlands and Spectacled Bear opportunities.

Must the days follow this exact order?

No. We recommend the full-day Mindo tour first, but any day may change according to weather, recent sightings, access, your targets and your travel route.

Are transportation and entrance fees included?

Yes. Transportation, a local guide, optics assistance and entrance fees are included. Meals are not included.

Can the fourth day start in Quito or Mindo?

Yes. The Andean Highlands day can be coordinated from Quito or Mindo and can follow an extended 5:00 AM–6:00 PM format or a shorter 6:00 AM–1:00 PM format.

Are target birds or bears guaranteed?

No. Wildlife is never guaranteed. Your guide selects the strongest realistic plan using recent observations, habitat, weather, season and available time.
