Tip Your Guide

If your guide helped make your time in Mindo memorable, you can leave an optional tip here. Choose the guide, enter the amount you would like to give, and continue to Stripe for secure payment.

The tip is separate from your tour payment and booking balance. It does not change the price or status of your reservation.

  • The checkout amount is charged in US dollars only.
  • Your guide’s email address is not requested.
  • The selected guide and tip purpose are recorded with the payment.
  • Stripe processes the card payment securely.

Choose your tip

US$5 is suggested. Enter a whole-dollar amount from US$1 to US$1,000.

You will review the USD amount on Stripe before paying. Tips are voluntary and non-refundable once distributed to the guide.

Who receives it?

The payment is identified as a guide gratuity and assigned to the guide you select.

What currency is used?

The checkout is created in USD with localized currency conversion disabled.

Need help?

If you are unsure which guide to choose, select Staff or contact Mindo Bird Watching before paying.
