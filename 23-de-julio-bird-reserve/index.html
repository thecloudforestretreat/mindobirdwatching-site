Why we keep this guide conservative

The locality is real; the public birding profile is thin

Public records support the 23 de Julio place name in the San Miguel de los Bancos area, but they do not establish a detailed reserve profile.

23 de Julio is not the same as 23 de Junio

A separate public birding destination called Recinto 23 de Junio exists in the same broader canton. We keep the two entities separate and do not transfer species, facilities or access claims between them.
