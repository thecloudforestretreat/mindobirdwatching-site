A trip shaped around birds, photography and changing habitats

Shannon joined Mindo Bird Watching for one full-day tour, one spectacled bear tour, three custom tours and one night walk. Together, these experiences connected cloud forest birding, high-Andean wildlife and nocturnal nature.
