Mindo after dark

A rainy walk and a tiny frog

My visit to Mindo and the surrounding area was a sheer delight, due in large part to the expert guides at Mindo Bird Watching. On a rainy night walk, we found tiny frogs peering from beneath flowers that looked like umbrellas.

The close attention required after dark changed the scale of the experience. Wet leaves, small movements and details that might disappear during the day became the focus.

Dawn in the cloud forest

Cock-of-the-Rock, toucans and hummingbirds

At dawn, an Andean Cock-of-the-Rock lek became a dramatic dance-off. Later came toucans clowning around and an iridescent rainbow of hummingbirds with the zoomies.

I stayed in Mindo for a week at eco-lodges at different elevations. The outings were a joy both as a nature lover and as a photographer, thanks to Neicer and Chicho's professionalism, local knowledge and passion for their work.

From cloud forest to páramo

A custom day in the high Andes

Near the end of the trip, I added a custom day with Neicer to Papallacta. Walking on springy páramo cushion plants felt like stepping into a fairy tale. We continued to search for birds, waterfalls and high-Andean wildlife.

A clear evening ended with the Milky Way visible to the naked eye. I cannot wait to return. My advice is to give Mindo more than a day: different elevations, unhurried time in the field and knowledgeable guides made it possible to experience the area's biodiversity with the attention it deserves.
