Questions about private and small group tours

Clear answers before you send your dates.

Can I book a private tour or join a small group?

Yes. You can reserve a private tour for your own party or request to join a compatible small group when space is available. Small groups are normally 2–8 guests; availability depends on dates, route, pace, and capacity.

Are your birding guides bilingual?

Yes. Our local guides are bilingual and use conversational English on tours. They regularly guide travelers from North America and Europe while sharing practical local knowledge of Mindo’s birds and cloud forest.

Can you plan around target birds or photography?

Yes. Share your target species, photography goals, and preferred pace. Sightings always depend on season, weather, and recent activity, but we plan around the most suitable habitats and timing.

How far in advance should I contact you?

Earlier is better for dawn routes, private guide availability, and compatible small-group space. If your trip is soon, contact us anyway and we will explain the realistic options.
