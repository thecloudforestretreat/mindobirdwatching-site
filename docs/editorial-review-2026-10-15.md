# Live main-site editorial and indexing repair

Scope: 36 English/Spanish pages on mindobirdwatching.com, related calendar assets, two relevant Spanish legacy redirects and two species sitemap entries. Staging configuration, authentication, analytics implementation and booking forms are outside this change.

## Evidence and editorial choices

- Reference: MBW_SiteMap, `sitemap_seo_enriched`, spreadsheet `1LJi_VxoNEOE7cK4_wgQJB8VCohI_-T6xq_IBvmy39v4`, rows 2–383, reviewed October 15, 2026. The sheet's technical “monitor” or “internal links only” actions do not constitute an editorial review. Its old rafting canonical instruction conflicts with current live evidence; the correct self-canonical was preserved.
- Calendar: the previous public Apps Script feed mismatched five photo/species pairs. Corrected Wattled Guan, Broad-billed Motmot, Powerful Woodpecker, Ornate Hawk-Eagle and Black Solitaire. The replacement Wattled Guan image is Cullen Hanks' photograph, [Commons source and species identification](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aburria_aburri_177963672.jpg), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), displayed without intentional alteration. Other existing photo-source links are retained. Species cards are now present in the HTML; search and category filters progressively enhance them. `assets/data/birding-calendar-species.json` records the corrected mapping and the HTML is its publishing surface.
- The old monthly species grades had no available dated methodology or guide approval. They were removed from the live calendar instead of represented as validated observations. The species collection remains; sightings are not promised. Guide validation of species-specific seasonal variation remains pending and must precede any reinstatement of grades.
- Monthly planning: preserve all 24 existing month URLs and English/Spanish pairs because the source sheet records some search traffic. Replace repeated general sales text with adjacent-month rainfall comparisons and a distinct planning task for each month. Add crawlable links from both calendar hubs and adjacent-month navigation. This is an editorial improvement, not a promise of indexation or ranking.
- Climate source: [NASA POWER climatology API documentation](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/services/api/temporal/climatology/), the public point response linked in `assets/data/mindo-climate-reference.json`, MERRA-2, 2001–2020, latitude −0.052, longitude −78.775. Only precipitation is used. Daily-average precipitation units are mm/day. This is a coarse mountainous regional grid, not a town station or a forecast; its model surface elevation is 1862.95 m, so temperature from this response was deliberately not presented as Mindo temperature. Regional comparisons do not establish bird-sighting likelihood. Route-planning advice is editorial advice derived from those stated constraints, not a claim of a guide's recorded experience.
- Homepages: remove internal search/intent/“built to rank” language while retaining link destinations and analytics attributes. Core Mindo tour collections gain a practical route comparison; Ecuador collections distinguish local outings from a wider itinerary; Mindo guides retain their useful practical blocks while reducing repetitive introductions. English/Spanish counterparts are edited together.
- Fix legacy `/es/tours-half-day[/]` to `/es/tours/medio-dia/` and `/es/tours/privado[/]` to `/es/tours/tour-personalizado-privado/` with 301s. Replace three stale contextual links discovered in the core pages. Add `/mindo-bird-species/` and `/es/especies-de-aves-de-mindo/` to both sitemap files with reciprocal alternates. Update lastmod only for changed URLs.
